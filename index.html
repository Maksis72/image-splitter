How the Image Splitter Works

The page splits your image into a grid of pages: the printable part of each page is the paper minus a 10 mm margin, the image is scaled to the chosen number of pages across and centered on as many rows as it needs. Each page is drawn at up to 200 DPI and saved into one PDF with cut marks and a page number. For the pieces, the original image is cut into equal parts at full resolution and packed into a ZIP.

The code is small and has no dependencies: tiles.ts (the grid math), pdf.ts (a tiny PDF writer), zip.ts (a ZIP writer with CRC-32) and halftone.ts (the dots). Read it, fork it or use it in your own project under the MIT license on GitHub.
